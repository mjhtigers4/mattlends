Keep your loan on track

The dos and don'ts of your mortgage.

Your credit, employment, and bank accounts can be reviewed again before closing. These simple habits can help keep your mortgage process on track.

✓   Do

  • ✓Keep paying every bill on time, especially rent.
  • ✓Keep your pay stubs, bank statements, and tax documents organized.
  • ✓Send every page of every bank statement, even blank pages.
  • ✓Respond to document requests as quickly as possible.
  • ✓Let me know before making major financial changes.

×   Don't

  • ×Open new credit cards or take out new loans.
  • ×Finance furniture, appliances, or a new vehicle.
  • ×Change or quit your job without talking to me first.
  • ×Move money around without keeping a paper trail.
  • ×Make large cash deposits or co-sign for someone else.
One important rule: If you're unsure whether something could affect your mortgage, call me before you do it. A quick conversation can save a lot of headaches later.
