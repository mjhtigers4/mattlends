A little mortgage advice

The dos and don'ts before closing.

Your credit, employment, and bank accounts can be reviewed again before closing. A few simple habits can help keep your mortgage on track.

✓   Do

  • ✓Pay every bill on time.
  • ✓Keep pay stubs and bank statements organized.
  • ✓Send every page of your statements, even blank ones.
  • ✓Respond to document requests promptly.
  • ✓Talk to me before making major financial changes.

×   Don't

  • ×Open new credit cards or take out new loans.
  • ×Finance furniture, appliances, or a new vehicle.
  • ×Change jobs without talking to me first.
  • ×Move money around without keeping a paper trail.
  • ×Make large cash deposits or co-sign for someone.
Not sure about something? Call me before you do it. A quick conversation can save a lot of headaches later.
