Keep your financial life boring until closing.

Your credit, income and bank accounts can be re-checked in the final days before closing. These simple habits keep your approval on track.

Do

  • Pay every bill on time, rent included.
  • Keep your pay stubs, bank statements and tax documents in one place.
  • Send every page of every statement, even blank ones.
  • Reply to document requests the same day when you can.
  • Tell me before anything financial changes.

Don’t

  • Open new credit cards or loans, or finance furniture or a car.
  • Change or quit your job without talking to me first.
  • Move money around without a paper trail.
  • Make large cash deposits.
  • Co-sign for anyone or close old accounts on your own.

Not sure about something? Call me before you do it.
